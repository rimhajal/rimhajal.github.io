## Research Interests
Include: optimization methods for machine learning, particularly **optimal transport**, **combinatorial optimization**, and continuous optimization techniques for **data selection and representation learning**. 

My work focuses on developing theoretically grounded and computationally efficient algorithms for **active learning** and **coreset selection**.

## Background
I did a double masters degree at the **University of Montpellier** that encompasses both Statistics-based Data Science and Business Management.

Before that, my bachelor's degree was in **pure maths** which I completed back home in **Beirut, Lebanon**.
